College Onboarding Kit

Everything to take a college from first meeting to live in 7 days — what to ask, what to collect, where to paste the code.

1 · The 90-minute sit-down (your CKP session)

Who to call into the room: Principal (decision maker) · Administrative Officer (fees/intake truth) · Placement Officer (placement data) · IQAC coordinator (if Module B is in scope) · office clerk with the current prospectus/fee file.
  • 0–10 min — Demo first. Open /widget/1 on your laptop/phone. Let the principal type a question in Telugu. Then have them try the "100% guarantee" trap. This sells the meeting — do it before any paperwork.
  • 10–45 min — Build the College Knowledge Pack live. You type into the dashboard (Section 2 worksheet below); they correct you. Say: "I'll do the typing sir, you just correct me."
  • 45–60 min — Approve the tone. Show 5 sample answers (fees, hostel, bus, placements, guarantee-refusal). Principal initials the approval line (Section 4).
  • 60–75 min — Integration. Get their webmaster on phone/WhatsApp; paste the one-line embed (Section 5). Test it live on their site.
  • 75–90 min — Follow-up machine. Connect their Meta lead form if any, set WhatsApp follow-up consent wording, agree the go-live date.

  • Latest prospectus / fee structure GO — the AI quotes exact fees from this

  • Placement letters or circulars (top 10 recruiters)
  • College logo (PNG/SVG) + 5 campus photos
  • Hostel & transport fee circular
  • Previous AQAR copy + NAAC certificate (if Module B)

5 · Website integration menu (even if they already have a website)

The one-liner (preferred — works with any site: custom PHP, Laravel, WordPress, Joomla, Wix):
<script src="https://YOUR-SEATSETU-HOST/embed.js" data-college="1"></script>

Paste once before </body> in the site template → a branded 💬 bubble appears on every page. Their webmaster needs 5 minutes. No redesign, no migration, nothing else changes on their site.

6 · Go-live checklist (day 4)

  • CKP approved & test chat passes the "suspicious parent" trial (fees, hostel, guarantee-trap)
  • embed.js live on their site + bubble tested on mobile
  • QR printed on at least the main hoarding/banner
  • Counselor's phone receives the first hand-off test (call routing confirmed)
  • Kill switch location shown to the principal; admin password handed over personally

    FAQ

    Will the AI give wrong information about fees or placements?

    No. It answers only from your approved College Knowledge Pack, cites the source, and refuses traps like "100% guarantee unda?" — protecting you under the Consumer Protection Act. Every answer is logged and you have a kill switch.

    Our staff is not technical. Will they use it?

    That's why v0.6 exists: each person gets only THEIR screens, lands directly on their daily work list, and a Help tab explains their job in plain English + Telugu. Counselors open one tab: their call list.

    How does it handle "compare with XYZ college" questions?

    You curate 2–3 nearby colleges' public numbers (fee, placements %, closing ranks + source). The AI shares those honestly, credits their strengths, states your edge, and converts the parent into a campus visit — instead of badmouthing anyone.

    Does the mock interview need ChatGPT/API keys?

    No. Question banks, adaptive follow-up probes, STAR scoring and even the spoken interviewer (browser voice) run locally — zero per-use cost, works without internet-grade servers.

    What do you need from us to go live?

    7 days: your facts (courses, fees, hostels, buses, placements), your logo, and a webmaster to paste one script line. We build the Knowledge Pack, train your staff and sit with you on launch day. Onboarding is done for you.
